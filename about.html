ABOUT THE FOUNDER

Adriell Wilson

Founder · Photographer · Videographer · Computer Science Student

My name is Adriell Wilson, and I started Wilson Unique Studios because I enjoy telling stories through photography and film. I'm currently studying Computer Science at the University of the Southern Caribbean, but I've also spent the past few years working with cameras, editing videos and covering different types of events.

I've worked on portraits, headshots, weddings, university events and media projects for businesses and organisations. What I like most is being able to combine the creative side of media with what I'm learning about technology.

I'm originally from Trinidad and grew up in Guyana, so working with people across the Caribbean is something that matters to me. I want the studio to keep growing while still making work that feels personal and true to the people involved.

HOW I WORK

Good work starts
with listening.

We understand that every client has a different story and budget. Some need clean headshots for a team. Others want their wedding documented, or a conference recorded for people who could not attend.

Our aim is to work closely with clients from planning to delivery, paying attention to the moments that make each project different.

We work across photography, video production and live event coverage, with additional services depending on what the project needs.
